Cafés in London

A small guide to two unique London cafés worth visiting.

Zombie Games Cafe & Bar

Zombie Games Cafe & Bar is a hidden gem for gaming enthusiasts, offering old consoles, board games, hot drinks, canned beverages, and gaming-themed cocktails.

There is no entry fee — just buy a coffee and enjoy access to the games. Its cosy interior, friendly atmosphere, and affordable prices make it a great relaxed spot away from central London.

Nearest station: Kilburn Underground Station, around a 10-minute walk away. You can also take the 32, 189, or 16 bus routes.

Golborne Deli & Wine Store

Golborne Deli & Wine Store may not be advertised as a café, but it has the charm of one. This Italian deli offers artisanal products, fresh pastries, daily meals, and outdoor seating.

Despite being closer to central London, it still feels relaxed and reasonably priced, making it a great place to stop after exploring the area.

Nearest station: Westbourne Park Station, around a 10-minute walk away. You can also take the 23 bus.
